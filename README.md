# Flashpoint Logistics

Production website for Flashpoint Logistics Ltd — a courier and logistics company operating across London. Built and deployed as part of an end-to-end digital infrastructure consultancy engagement.

Live site: [flashpointlogistics.co.uk](https://flashpointlogistics.co.uk)

---

## What this repo contains

Full production codebase for the Flashpoint Logistics website, including lead generation infrastructure, email automation pipeline, and SEO implementation.

---

## What was built

### Website
- Built from scratch in **HTML and CSS**, fully responsive across all device sizes
- Hosted on **GitHub Pages** with custom domain configured via **GoDaddy**
- Clean, professional design optimised for conversion

### Lead generation pipeline
- Dual **Formspree** and **EmailJS** form integration
- Every enquiry submission triggers a branded HTML email notification delivered directly to the business inbox
- No third-party CRM required at launch — keeps the stack lean for a new business

### Email authentication
- **SPF, DKIM, and DMARC** configured end-to-end
- **Outlook integration** via EmailJS for transactional delivery
- Branded HTML email templates for all outbound communications

### SEO infrastructure
- Schema markup (LocalBusiness structured data)
- Open Graph tags for social sharing
- Local borough keyword targeting for London courier search terms
- sitemap.xml and robots.txt configured
- **Google Search Console** verified and indexed
- **Google Business Profile** set up, verified, and live on Google Maps

### Legal and compliance
- GDPR-compliant Privacy Policy drafted with UK-specific data handling clauses
- Terms and Conditions drafted with logistics liability provisions

## Automation roadmap (advisory)

A phased automation roadmap was scoped and delivered as part of the engagement, tiered by business maturity:

- **Phase 1 (immediate):** Quote acknowledgement emails, enquiry logging to Google Sheets
- **Phase 2 (3 to 6 months):** Job tracking workflow, automated invoice generation
- **Phase 3 (6 to 12 months):** Driver dispatch notifications, customer delivery updates
